Try Mythree

Speak in your own language, and in a few seconds hear yourself speak another one: English, Hindi, Spanish, Japanese… 102 languages, all 22 of India's, and 94 of them even in your own voice.

Use a phone or computer with a microphone, in Chrome or Safari, somewhere quiet.

1 Speak, and hear it in any language

  1. Tap the green button once. It turns red: Mythree is listening.
  2. Say one or two sentences in your language.
  3. Tap the red button when you're done.
  4. In a few seconds you'll read it in the language you chose and hear it. Tap 🔁 to hear it again.

Ideas: tell your mother you miss her. Say a proverb from your language. Describe your favourite food. Say what you're building at the hackathon.

Tap to start talking

The microphone doesn't work?

iPhone: when Safari asks "Allow microphone?", tap Allow. If you said no before: Settings → Safari → Microphone → Allow, then reload the page.

Android or computer (Chrome): tap the small icon left of the web address → Microphone → Allow, then reload.

Did you like it? Now hear it in your own voice: Mythree learns how you sound in 20 seconds.

    2 Lend Mythree your voice

    Mythree listens to you for 20 seconds and learns how you sound. Then it speaks as you, in the language you chose.

    This is a hackathon test: Mythree can lend its own-voice magic to 7 visitors at a time. If they're all in use, you'll hear a standard voice instead, and everything else works the same. Please tap "Done" when you finish, so the next person can have your place.

    When you tap Start, read this aloud, warmly, like you're talking to a friend. Mythree stops by itself after 20 seconds.
